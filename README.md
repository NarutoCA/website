## Topic 1: Home Coffee Brewing

![Coffee cup placeholder](https://example.com/images/coffee.jpg)

Great coffee at home comes down to four things: **fresh beans, the right grind, good water, and a consistent ratio.**

### Brewing methods at a glance

| Method | Grind | Brew time | Flavor profile |
|--------|-------|-----------|----------------|
| French press | Coarse | 4 minutes | Full-bodied, rich |
| Pour-over | Medium | 2.5-3 minutes | Clean, bright |
| AeroPress | Medium-fine | 1-2 minutes | Smooth, versatile |
| Moka pot | Fine | 5 minutes | Strong, espresso-like |

### Quick tips

1. **Buy whole beans** and grind just before brewing.
2. **Weigh your coffee.** A good starting ratio is 1 gram of coffee to 16 grams of water.
3. **Use water just off the boil**, around 92-96 °C (195-205 °F).
4. **Keep your gear clean.** Old oils make coffee taste bitter.

> "Coffee is a language in itself." — *A common saying among baristas*

## Topic 2: Urban Gardening

![Balcony garden placeholder](https://example.com/images/garden.jpg)

You don't need a backyard to grow your own food. A sunny balcony, a windowsill, or even a shared community plot is enough to get started.

### Easy plants for beginners

- 🌿 **Herbs:** basil, mint, chives, parsley
- 🍅 **Vegetables:** cherry tomatoes, lettuce, radishes
- 🍓 **Fruit:** strawberries in hanging baskets
- 🌸 **Flowers:** nasturtiums and marigolds (edible and pest-repelling)

### Starter checklist

- [x] Find a spot with at least 6 hours of sun
- [x] Choose containers with drainage holes
- [ ] Buy quality potting mix (not garden soil)
- [ ] Pick 3 plants to start with
- [ ] Set a simple watering reminder

### Seasonal planting guide

| Season | What to plant | Notes |
|--------|---------------|-------|
| Spring | Lettuce, peas, radishes | Cool-weather crops thrive |
| Summer | Tomatoes, basil, peppers | Water daily in heat |
| Fall | Kale, garlic, spinach | Garlic overwinters well |
| Winter | Microgreens (indoors) | Grow under a simple light |

## Topic 3: Hiking in the Pacific Northwest

![Mountain trail placeholder](https://example.com/images/hiking.jpg)

From coastal rainforest to alpine ridgelines, the Pacific Northwest offers trails for every level. Plan ahead, pack smart, and leave no trace.

### Trail difficulty guide

| Level | Distance | Elevation gain | Example experience |
|-------|----------|----------------|--------------------|
| Easy | 2-5 km | Under 150 m | Forest and shoreline walks |
| Moderate | 6-12 km | 300-600 m | Lake and viewpoint hikes |
| Challenging | 12+ km | 800 m+ | Summit and ridge routes |

### The Ten Essentials (simplified)

1. Navigation (map, compass, or GPS)
2. Headlamp
3. Sun protection
4. First aid kit
5. Knife or multi-tool
6. Fire starter
7. Emergency shelter
8. Extra food
9. Extra water
10. Extra clothing

### Trail etiquette

- Stay on marked trails to protect vegetation.
- Yield to uphill hikers.
- Pack out everything you pack in.
- Keep a respectful distance from wildlife.

```text
Before you go:
  1. Check the weather forecast
  2. Check trail conditions and closures
  3. Tell someone your route and return time
```
